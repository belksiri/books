v1.0.1 — September 21, 2026 · Volume 2 only

The chapter numbering was added to the archive. The folders of Volume 2 carry the internal identifiers CH27 to CH42, which are not chapter numbers: the volume is read as Chapter 1 through Chapter 16. The archive now states the correspondence, in its README.txt and in its MANIFEST.json.

No flowgraph changed. The 16 resource files are byte-for-byte the ones of v1.0.0, with the same individual checksums. Only the two description files inside the archive differ, so only the checksum of the archive itself moved — from f5316a492d9df4f3bd367b001616807c0a436e694c81787b0eceb0b1e3589a90 (77,004 bytes) to d7a6b1727fd9d4ad5e7e9a536e039e3423684a7a1416865a3c60b579c680f0c7 (78,372 bytes).

If you downloaded Volume 2 before September 21, 2026, you do not need to download it again: your flowgraphs are current. Fetch the new archive only if you want the chapter-numbering table.

The Volume 1 archive was not reissued and stays at v1.0.0.

The same files in every language

The reader resources are not translated, and they are not duplicated. A .grc flowgraph holds official GNU Radio block names, numeric values and stable identifiers; none of it depends on the language of the book. The English edition and the French edition therefore download the same two archives, under the same names, with the same checksums.

A correction to the resources applies to every edition at once: there is one set of files, one version number, and one checksum per archive. The French corrections page tracks the same releases.

Report a problem

A flowgraph that refuses to open, a missing file, a checksum that does not match: open an issue on GitHub, giving the volume, the path of the file and your version of GNU Radio.

What is not published here

This page distributes only the files the reader needs. Not published: the text of the book, the composition files, the covers, the working documents, and the Python programs that GNU Radio Companion produces itself — those are rebuilt at every generation.

Two hands-on exercise files are not provided either, because the reader is the one who produces them: echantillons.iq (Exercise 8) and the .cfile of real samples (Exercise 18).
