One archive, every edition

The resources below are the same in every language: a .grc file holds official GNU Radio block names, numeric values and stable identifiers, none of which depends on the language of the book. They are the same files the French edition uses, under the same names and with the same checksums — which is why the archive keeps its French file name.

Verify your download

The SHA-256 checksum of the archive must be exactly:

d7a6b1727fd9d4ad5e7e9a536e039e3423684a7a1416865a3c60b579c680f0c7

The checksums of every file, one by one, are in the volume manifest and in SHA256SUMS.

Chapters that need a receiver

Unlike Volume 1, this volume uses a real receiver in its last part. The measurements published in the book were made with an RTL2832U dongle and an R820T tuner actually plugged in, antenna connected, real transmissions actually received. The RTL-SDR Blog V3, the reference model of the book, was not tried, and no listening test was validated during those sessions.
